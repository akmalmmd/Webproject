Our Story

Cultivating community and healthy living since 2010.

Rooted in Tradition

Green Roots Farm started with a simple belief: food should be grown with respect for the earth and the people who eat it. What began as a small one-acre plot tended by founders Sarah and David has blossomed into a thriving 50-acre organic farm.

We left our corporate jobs in the city, seeking a deeper connection to nature and a healthier lifestyle for our growing family. That passion translates into every carrot pulled and every tomato picked.

Our Philosophy

We practice regenerative agriculture. This means we go beyond just "sustainable" – we actively work to improve soil health, increase biodiversity, and enhance the ecosystem of our farm.

  • No Synthetic Chemicals: We use natural compost and beneficial insects.
  • Crop Rotation: Keeping the soil rich in nutrients year after year.
  • Water Conservation: Utilizing smart drip irrigation systems.

Our Growing Standards

Soil Health First

Healthy plants start with healthy soil. We use cover crops and minimal tilling to keep the microbiology of our soil thriving.

Pest Management

We rely on beneficial insects, crop rotation, and physical barriers rather than harmful chemical sprays to manage pests.
